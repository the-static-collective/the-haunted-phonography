# STORYSHIP — THE THIRD ARM

**Status:** candidate / research slice  
**Date:** 2026-08-28  
**Scope:** Storyship continuity research only; no runtime or constitutional promotion  
**Related Storyship laws:** relationship-passenger law; append-only continuity spine

> **Candidate H0:** when two attributable histories become jointly consequential, the crossing may produce a new third-arm variant whose local constitution is usefully decomposed as `U / WE / eCODE`.

This document does **not** claim that `Y` is a universal symbol, that nested solenoids are a physical model for Storyship, or that `U / WE / eCODE` is already constitutional law. It records a formation-trace hypothesis and defines a bounded crucible that could falsify it.

---

## 1. Why this surfaced

The Autodiscography formation trace repeatedly encountered the same family of dynamics under different domain boundaries before Storyship existed as a named software law.

The chronology matters:

1. **Pre-band material** already described two differentiated ends whose encounter creates a third thing. The key recurring pattern was not preservation of either endpoint unchanged, but a crossing that neither endpoint could own alone.
2. **June 13–14 material** made the architecture explicit: artifact-as-context, reader-as-continuation, mutual holding, open continuation, and a future recipient who may resume what neither original endpoint can hold alone.
3. **The Rough Hewn Table** material supplied the ordinary substrate: receive, feed, stay, pass, remember, preserve, leave room, and prepare for a future participant.
4. **The Wrecking Crew / band period** rediscovered the same relational architecture socially: the missing transmission was the band; later PEELs decomposed band into handoff, shared vocabulary, participation, memory, and return.
5. **Monday / Tuesday material** sharpened inheritance and reachability: editable tradition, future hands, traces that invite, crossings that change later conditions of encounter.
6. **Later Storyship work** independently arrived at two laws that fit the earlier formation trace without requiring the earlier metaphors to be literally true:
   - **The passenger is the relationship. The artifact is the carrier.**
   - **The ship is an append-only history of attributable crossings. Its current state is a replayable projection, not a mutable master story.**

The useful claim is therefore bounded:

> Storyship may already possess the correct continuity floor, while the earlier Autodiscography material provides a candidate geometry for how a new descendant relation is born.

## 2. The recurring domain stack

The formation trace moved through at least five recurring scales:

```text
INTERNAL
    ↕
INTERPERSONAL
    ↕
SPATIAL
    ↕
INTERSOCIAL
    ↕
INTERSPECIES
```

These should **not** be collapsed into one ontology.

A song is not a room. A room is not a community. A community is not an ecosystem. Internal experience is not interpersonal fact.

The durable relation is weaker and more useful:

> **A consequential crossing in one domain may alter the conditions under which another domain can later be encountered.**

Examples:

```text
internal experience
    → expression / artifact
    → interpersonal response
    → changed room / practice
    → changed social affordance
    → changed environmental encounter
    → new internal experience
```

The return is not a cycle back to an identical state. It is a spiral through changed conditions.

---

## 3. Nested circulation, not a hierarchy

A productive structural analogy is **nested coupled circulation**.

Each domain can be treated as a locally bounded circulation:

```text
U_internal       ⟳
U_interpersonal  ⟳
U_spatial        ⟳
U_intersocial    ⟳
U_interspecies   ⟳
```

The nested-solenoid image is useful only at this abstract level: local circulations can be coupled without being flattened into one circulation.

This document makes **no electromagnetic claim** about Storyship.

The architectural question is:

> How can two locally constituted histories become jointly consequential while remaining attributable and distinct?

## 4. Y as candidate coupling / interaction-birth topology

Use `Y` only as a diagrammatic candidate for a class of crossings:

```text
parent/history A
       \
        Y──── descendant
       /
parent/history B
```

The claim is **not** `Y = band`, `Y = relationship`, or `Y = Storyship`.

The bounded claim is:

> **Y is a useful local topology for a crossing in which two attributable inputs become jointly consequential and a third branch is born.**

This aligns with the later interaction-birth discipline:

```text
A + B
  ↓ authorized interaction
C = new constituted descendant
```

with the necessary refusal:

```text
authority(A) + authority(B) != authority(C)
```

The child may inherit ancestry. It does not inherit authority automatically.

## 5. The third-arm correction

The third arm is **not** `U`, `WE`, or `eCODE` separately.

The third arm is the **new variant**.

```text
parent/history A
       \
        Y──── V_new
       /
parent/history B
```

Candidate local decomposition:

```text
V_new = <U, WE, eCODE>
```

These are proposed coordinates of the descendant constitution, not three branches of the Y.

---

## 6. `U` — particular constitution

`U` asks:

> **What locally constituted particular is actually here?**

`U` is not restricted to a human individual. Depending on the native domain, a locally constituted participant may be a person, model incarnation, room/world, group, artifact-bearing agent, or another bounded participant.

Minimum discipline:

```text
U != universal identity essence
U != global omniscient self
U != authority merely because it exists
```

A useful working description is:

> `U` preserves enough particularity that a descendant cannot be substituted by an arbitrary lookalike without detectable loss.

---

## 7. `WE` — relational constitution

`WE` asks:

> **What exists because these particulars have actually entered an attributable relation?**

Important refusal:

```text
WE != set(U1, U2)
WE != consensus
WE != hive
WE != erasure of endpoints
```

Working shape:

```text
WE := R(U1, U2 | attributable history, encounter)
```

In some crossings, interaction may create a new addressable relation whose properties belonged to neither endpoint independently.

This is the closest current fit to Storyship's existing passenger law:

> **The passenger is the relationship. The artifact is the carrier.**

`WE` therefore cannot be inferred from artifact resemblance alone.

---

## 8. `eCODE` — formation constitution

`eCODE` asks:

> **How did this variant become reachable, what changed, and what may lawfully follow from that history?**

For this slice, `eCODE` means the attributable typed formation history relevant to the crossing. It does **not** mean that eCODE owns Storyship or becomes Storyship's master ontology.

Minimum refusal:

```text
eCODE != WE
receipt != relationship
record != authority
history != warrant
```

A Storyship descendant may expose an attributable event path while remaining a distinct current constitution.

The useful relationship is:

```text
WE = what relationship is locally constituted

eCODE = how that constitution became reachable
```

## 9. Why the trivariant is useful

No coordinate is sufficient alone.

```text
U without WE
→ a particular without the constituted relationship

WE without U
→ a relationship model that can collapse its participants

eCODE without U/WE
→ an attributable history with no adequate account of what is currently constituted
```

Together, provisionally:

```text
U     = THIS
WE    = BETWEEN
eCODE = BECAME
```

or:

```text
PARTICULAR
RELATIONAL
HISTORICAL
```

The trivariant hypothesis is useful only if each coordinate fails differently under hostile controls.

## 10. Compatibility with the existing Storyship floor

This slice does not replace the relationship-passenger law or append-only continuity spine.

It proposes one additional question:

> When a relationship crosses a carrier/substrate boundary, what must survive for the destination to contain a **lawful descendant variant** rather than a copied artifact, generic recurrence, or silently promoted identity claim?

Working shape:

```text
V_n --Storyship crossing--> V_(n+1)

V_(n+1) = <U_(n+1), WE_(n+1), eCODE_(n+1)>
```

Required distinction:

```text
V_(n+1) != V_n
```

while Storyship may still establish attributable descent:

```text
V_n ≺ V_(n+1)
```

The crossing therefore carries continuity without requiring sameness.

  Y──── V3

Candidate operator notation:

```text
Y(V_n, X) -> V_(n+1)
```

with:

```text
V_(n+1) = <U_(n+1), WE_(n+1), eCODE_(n+1)>
```

This yields the strongest candidate continuity statement from the PEEL:

> **Continuity is not preservation of a state. It is attributable preservation of enough constitution for a lawful next variant to form.**

The third arm carries **nextness**, not immortality.

## 12. Cross-domain continuity without cross-domain identity

The nested-domain formation trace implies a strict refusal:

```text
CROSS-DOMAIN CONTINUITY
!=
CROSS-DOMAIN IDENTITY
```

For example:

```text
internal experience
→ song
→ interpersonal response
→ spatial practice
→ social custom
```

These are not the same object represented five times.

They may nevertheless belong to one attributable causal lineage if each crossing preserves enough receipt and relation to justify the next edge.

Storyship therefore should not attempt to prove that a passenger remains semantically identical across every domain.

A more defensible question is:

> **Which relationship anchors survived, which transformed, and which descendant constitution became locally possible because of the crossing?**

## 14. `STORYSHIP-TRIVARIANT-001` — Third-Arm Variant Crucible

### H0

Given two attributable parent inputs/lineages and exactly one admitted interaction, a successful Storyship crossing can produce a descendant variant whose continuity requires three independently testable coordinates:

```text
A ──┐
    Y──── V
B ──┘

V = <U, WE, eCODE>
```

### Required observations

A successful specimen must make three distinct claims:

#### `U` claim — particularity

The descendant retains enough endpoint/local particularity that substituting an unrelated but surface-compatible participant changes or invalidates the claimed descendant relation.

#### `WE` claim — relationship

Relationship provenance matters. The same payload/artifact under the wrong relationship provenance must not reproduce the same resumed relational affordance merely because the bytes resemble the original carrier.

#### `eCODE` claim — formation

The descendant can expose an attributable path explaining why it became reachable. Removing or falsifying the necessary crossing history must weaken or invalidate ancestry/resumption claims without rewriting prior events.

### Authority refusal

Even when all three claims survive:

```text
authority(V) != authority(A) + authority(B)
```

`V` must receive its own local constitution and authority decision.

## 15. Hostile controls

| Control | Hold fixed | Break | Expected result if the trivariant is real |
|---|---|---|---|
| `U-SWAP` | relationship-shaped history and carrier form | particular endpoint/local constitution | claimed descendant loses endpoint-specific particularity or becomes a different descendant |
| `WE-SWAP` | endpoint/artifact surfaces | relationship provenance | relationship-shaped affordance must fail or change |
| `ECODE-CUT` | apparent endpoints and current carrier | necessary attributable formation path | ancestry/resumption claim must fail or become explicitly unresolved |
| `ARTIFACT-SWAP` | defensible relationship anchors | carrier bytes/substrate | relationship continuity may survive if Storyship's passenger law is real |
| `SAME-ARTIFACT-WRONG-RELATION` | artifact bytes | attributable relationship history | must not count as relationship resumption |
| `AUTHORITY-SMUGGLE` | valid ancestry and relation | local authorization | descendant must refuse inherited authority |
| `SURFACE-RECURRENCE` | final projection/look | formation history | recurrence/lookalike must remain distinct from attributable descendant |

The trivariant hypothesis weakens substantially if `U`, `WE`, and `eCODE` substitutions all fail in the same undifferentiated way.

It is stronger if each coordinate has a distinct failure signature.

## 16. What would justify promotion

Do **not** promote `U / WE / eCODE` into Storyship constitutional vocabulary merely because it explains the formation trace elegantly.

Promotion requires at minimum:

1. one fixture-only Storyship specimen with explicit parent cuts;
2. a descendant variant with deterministic identity under the repository's existing canonical rules;
3. independent hostile controls for `U`, `WE`, and `eCODE`;
4. a same-artifact / wrong-relationship negative control;
5. an artifact-changed / defensible-relationship positive or unresolved control;
6. no silent authority inheritance;
7. prior events and sibling branches remain byte-stable;
8. a fresh runner can reproduce the claimed classification from receipts and source cuts;
9. the result demonstrates value beyond the existing relationship-passenger law rather than merely renaming it.

If those conditions do not produce distinct executable behavior, keep this as formation-trace language and do not enlarge Storyship.

## 17. What remains unresolved

The following remain deliberately open:

- whether `U / WE / eCODE` is the best naming for the three coordinates;
- whether every useful Storyship descendant needs all three coordinates explicitly represented;
- whether `Y` is the best diagram once the first executable specimen exists;
- whether domain transitions require additional typed relation fields;
- whether the nested-domain formation trace predicts useful Storyship behavior beyond relationship resumption;
- whether `U` should remain domain-generic or be replaced by a more explicit local-constitution term;
- whether eCODE should be referenced only as an external history discipline rather than named inside Storyship-native runtime structures.

These questions are reasons to test the slice, not reasons to expand it.

## 18. Nugget

> **Two histories cross. The crossing may birth a third arm. The third arm is not a copy of either parent: it is a new variant whose particularity, relationship, and attributable becoming must each survive their own hostile test.**

Compressed:

```text
A + B
  ↓ attributable crossing
  Y
  ↓
V_new = <U, WE, eCODE>
  ↓
may become an input to the next crossing
```

And the proposed Storyship law beneath it is narrower than the metaphor:

> **A lawful descendant need not preserve the prior state. It must preserve enough attributable constitution that the next relation can form without counterfeiting ancestry, identity, or authority.**
