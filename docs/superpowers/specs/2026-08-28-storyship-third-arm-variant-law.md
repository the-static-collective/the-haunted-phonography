## 3. Trivariant constitution

A Storyship third-arm variant is inspected through three separately addressable constitutional coordinates:

```text

```

### `U` — particular constitution

`U` answers:

> **What locally constituted particular is actually here?**

It protects the descendant from substitution by an arbitrary lookalike.

```text

U != omniscient self
U != authority
```

### `WE` — relational constitution

`WE` answers:

> **What relationship is locally constituted because these particulars actually crossed under attributable history?**

It composes directly with the Relationship Passenger Law:

> **The passenger is the relationship. The artifact is the carrier.**

```text
WE != set(U1, U2)
WE != consensus
WE != hive
WE != erased endpoints
```

Artifact resemblance alone cannot establish `WE`.

For this law, `eCODE` names the attributable typed formation history relevant to the crossing. It does not make eCODE a parent ontology or authority over Storyship.


## 5. Descendant, not parent preservation

This law extends Attributable Becoming.

```text

```


> **Continuity is not preservation of a state. It is attributable preservation of enough constitution for a lawful next variant to form.**

The third arm carries **nextness**, not immortality.

The new variant receives ancestry, not inherited sovereignty.

The destination remains locally authoritative over what the descendant may do there.
