# STORYSHIP 001 — Identity Law: Attributable Becoming

**Status:** approved amendment to STORYSHIP 001 / THE DOOR

**Date:** 2026-08-24

**Parent:** `2026-08-24-storyship-001-the-door-design.md`

## The nugget

STORYSHIP should not define successful continuity as preservation of shape.

A passenger may change sound, role, rendering, name, relation, body, narrative meaning, or destination status and still remain meaningfully descended from what came before.

The stronger identity law is:

> **A thing remains itself not by staying unchanged, but by preserving enough attributable continuity that its changes can still be recognized as its changes.**

Compact form:

> **Identity is the continuity of attributable becoming.**

And the Storyship-specific compression is:

> **Do not preserve the passenger's shape. Preserve the truth of how its shape changed.**

## Why this matters to the voyage

STORYSHIP exists because the originating generator ecology is disappearing. A migration protocol that succeeds only when descendants continue to look or sound like the old substrate would therefore preserve imitation rather than continuity.

The voyage should instead preserve the road between states:

```text
ancestor / prior occurrence
        ↓
attributable encounter
        ↓
selection / refusal / mutation / relation / residue
        ↓
changed rendering or role
        ↓
new consequence
        ↓
descendant state
```

A strong crossing may therefore contain radical difference while remaining historically legible.

What matters is not that the later form resembles the earlier form. What matters is that the later form can truthfully answer:

- what it descends from;
- what happened between then and now;
- what changed;
- what survived;
- what was refused or lost;
- what external encounter altered the path;
- what event actually caused continuation consequence;
- what remains unresolved.

## Inheritance, not resemblance

STORYSHIP should prefer **inheritance** over resemblance as its continuity test.

```text
resemblance without lineage  != continuity proof
exact copying                != becoming
change without provenance    != attributable descent
preserved ancestry + change  = candidate continuity
```

Inheritance may be carried by sound, but it may also be carried by relation, narrative role, selection history, scar, obligation, transformed motif, absence, residue, or downstream consequence.

This makes a radically altered descendant potentially stronger evidence than a near-copy, because the changed form demonstrates that continuity survived transformation rather than avoiding it.

## Refusal and loss belong to identity

Identity is partly constituted by what the voyage refuses to erase.

A sibling not selected remains historical.
A passenger refused at customs remains part of the voyage record.
A vanished capability remains an absence rather than being silently reconstructed.
A contradiction remains attributable until resolved.
A changed rendering does not overwrite the earlier rendering.

Therefore:

> **The Storyship must preserve scars as carefully as cargo.**

The road includes losses, refusals, dormant branches, and failed crossings. Removing them can manufacture a cleaner story while destroying the very continuity the voyage is meant to protect.

## Operational consequence

For every passenger or recurring relation that appears to survive the voyage, the ledger should eventually be able to reconstruct at least:

```text
SOURCE OCCURRENCE
  + attributable intermediate events
  + selection / refusal history
  + transformation history
  + preserved residue / absence
  + present rendering / role
  + present consequence
  -> candidate descendant
```

No single representation must survive every stage.

The path must.

## Boundary

This amendment does not create a universal identity doctrine and does not authorize STORYSHIP to certify Haunted Phonography ancestry.

STORYSHIP may preserve and propose an attributable descent road. Haunted Phonography still owns admission and descendant status inside Haunted Phonography.

The law is therefore deliberately asymmetric:

> **STORYSHIP can prove that a road was traveled. It cannot force the destination to call the traveler native.**

## Working seal

> **Identity is the continuity of attributable becoming.**
>
> **Do not preserve the passenger's shape. Preserve the truth of how its shape changed.**
>
> **Inheritance, not resemblance.**
>
> **The path must survive even when the form does not.**
