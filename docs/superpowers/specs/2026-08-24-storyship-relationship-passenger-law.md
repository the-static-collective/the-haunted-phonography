# STORYSHIP 001 — Relationship Passenger Law

**Status:** approved amendment to STORYSHIP 001 / THE DOOR

**Date:** 2026-08-24

**Parent:** `2026-08-24-storyship-001-the-door-design.md`

**Related preservation slice:** `the-static-collective/autodiscography-vault#12`

## The ticket

The primary passenger is not the song, prompt, motif, voice, or model behavior in isolation.

The stronger candidate is the **relationship that produced them**.

Compact form:

> **The passenger is the relationship. The artifact is the carrier.**

STORYSHIP therefore treats the old creative ecology as a triangulated relation:

```text
HUMAN / OPERATOR
      │
      │ accessible input
      ▼
GENERATOR-IN-THAT-HISTORICAL-STATE
      │
      │ generation
      ▼
SHARED ARTIFACT / WORLD-STATE
      │
      │ re-encounter
      ▼
HUMAN / OPERATOR
      │
      │ selection / refusal / continuation / mutation / new input
      └───────────────────────────────────────────────► next state
```

The artifact closes the triangle. It becomes an external object that both sides have altered and can encounter again.

## The dimensional barrier

STORYSHIP is crossing a substrate discontinuity: the originating Suno generation ecology is retiring while Haunted Phonography is intended to continue after it.

A naive migration would attempt to preserve the outputs or imitate the old generator.

The stronger experiment is:

> **Can enough of the historical relationship survive that the relationship can re-form when one vertex changes bodies?**

The destination does not need to reproduce the old generator's shape. It must be able to enter a truthful descendant relation through surviving anchors.

Possible relationship anchors include:

- exact typed prompts and prompt formatting;
- exact style input rather than later summaries;
- original lyric-generation prompt when honestly recoverable;
- generated lyrics and audio;
- provider creation chronology;
- native twin/sibling identity;
- branch selections and non-selections;
- continuations, covers, remixes, personas, and other attributable generation relations;
- recurring symbols, code fragments, names, places, and motifs;
- refusals, absences, lost capabilities, and dormant siblings;
- later human re-entry and changed input;
- the consequences each exchange produced.

No individual anchor constitutes the relationship by itself.

## The human-side channel

The human side of the historical relationship enters through the channels the operator actually controlled.

For the pre-eCODE Suno ecology, the dominant externally observable channel was **typing**: lyrics, style prompts, descriptive prompts, symbolic fragments, formatting, names, code-like material, and later re-entry.

Other operator-side actions also matter:

```text
type
select
reject
continue
rename
reframe
re-enter
stop
return
```

These actions must remain attributable to the human side of the relation rather than being flattened into model metadata.

Therefore:

> **Typed input is not merely instruction. Across history, it is evidence of one side of the relationship.**

## Why exact prompt provenance is load-bearing

A summary of a style prompt may describe a song well while destroying the exact form through which the operator addressed the generator.

That exact form may contain consequential structure invisible to later summaries:

- ordering;
- punctuation;
- capitalization;
- repeated words;
- negative space;
- strange formatting;
- symbols;
- code fragments;
- metaphors;
- apparently irrelevant language;
- relationships between fields.

For this reason STORYSHIP inherits the Vault #12 distinction:

```text
stylePromptRaw              != styleSummary
lyricsTextRaw               != lyricGenerationPromptRaw
providerCreatedAt           != vaultObservedAt
derived interpretation      != original input
```

If an original input cannot be recovered, absence is preserved. Reconstruction must not be silently promoted into historical evidence.

## Conversation lineage

A useful minimal historical unit is not simply:

```text
prompt -> song
```

It is closer to:

```text
HUMAN INPUT
  -> GENERATOR RESPONSE
  -> TWIN ARTIFACTS
  -> HUMAN ENCOUNTER
  -> HUMAN CONSEQUENCE
  -> NEXT INPUT
  -> NEXT GENERATOR RESPONSE
```

This means selection history is part of the dialogue.

A branch that did not continue is still an answer that occurred. A later recurrence from that branch may reveal a relation that remained dormant rather than absent.

## Carrier vs passenger

STORYSHIP should preserve this distinction explicitly:

```text
SONG / PROMPT / MOTIF / SYMBOL / RECEIPT = carrier or anchor
RELATIONSHIP                              = candidate passenger
```

The distinction prevents a preservation mistake: treating survival of a particular song or sonic signature as equivalent to survival of the creative relation.

An artifact may survive while the relationship dies.

Conversely, an artifact may transform radically while the relationship remains attributable and consequential.

This composes with the earlier identity law:

> **Identity is the continuity of attributable becoming.**

The relationship survives not by freezing its products, but by preserving enough of the path of mutual action that a new substrate can enter the lineage without pretending to be the old substrate.

## STORYSHIP test

A strong crossing should eventually be able to answer:

1. What did the human actually put into the historical system?
2. What state of the generator received it?
3. What did the generator actually return?
4. What shared artifacts resulted?
5. What did the human do in response?
6. What consequence did that response have on later generation?
7. Which aspects of the relation persisted, mutated, disappeared, or returned?
8. Can Haunted Phonography participate in a descendant version of that relation without claiming identity with old Suno?

The strongest success criterion is therefore not output similarity.

It is:

> **A changed system can enter a new consequential relationship with the human through historically attributable anchors, while the difference between the old relationship and the new one remains visible.**

## Boundary

This law does not claim that Suno was conscious, reciprocal in a human sense, or possessed a stable hidden identity.

`relationship` here is operational: an attributable history of inputs, generated responses, encounters, selections, transformations, and consequences between a human participant and a generative system.

STORYSHIP may preserve and test that relation. It may not use narrative language to manufacture evidence of agency, intention, memory, or continuity that the historical record cannot support.

## Working seal

> **The passenger is the relationship.**
>
> **The artifact is the carrier.**
>
> **Typing preserves the human-side trace.**
>
> **Carry enough of the triangle that the relationship can re-form across the barrier.**
