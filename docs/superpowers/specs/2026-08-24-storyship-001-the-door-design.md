# STORYSHIP 001 — THE DOOR

**Status:** approved architectural design / pre-implementation spec

**Date:** 2026-08-24

**Role:** flagship narrative transfer packet from the retiring Suno generation ecology into Haunted Phonography

**Motto:** **The story is true; the ship is how it travels.**

## 1. Why this exists

A finite Suno generation budget remains while the older model lineage that produced the pre-eCODE Autodiscography corpus approaches retirement.

The remaining budget should not be treated as a pile of credits to exhaust, nor as an attempt to reproduce the archive. The Vault is responsible for preserving the historical population. STORYSHIP 001 uses the remaining live generation access for the thing the archive cannot reconstruct later:

> **a final, reality-grounded migration process in which recurring musical, narrative, relational, temporal, symbolic, and behavioral material is given room to cross into a new substrate without being told in advance exactly what must survive.**

The destination is Haunted Phonography, but Haunted Phonography remains locally authoritative over what it admits. STORYSHIP may carry, propose, transform, remember, and arrive. It may not declare that a passenger has become native merely because the narrative says so.

## 2. The ship metaphor is operational

`THE DOOR` is intentionally a temporary carrier.

It is not the destination, not a permanent runtime, and not a new constitutional authority. Its job is to hold enough continuity above water while the originating generator ecology disappears behind it.

```text
Suno live generation ecology
        ↓
STORYSHIP 001 / THE DOOR
        ↓
reality-grounded narrative crossing
        ↓
Haunted Phonography customs
        ↓
admitted descendants / refused cargo / residue / dormant ancestry
```

The project should be considered successful even if some cargo does not cross.

Failure, refusal, loss, mutation, and unexpected stowaways are first-class outcomes.

## 3. Founding law: Reality / Narrative Braid

STORYSHIP has two simultaneous ledgers.

### 3.1 Reality ledger

The reality ledger records what actually happened and must remain literal where observable:

- Suno generation / song IDs;
- observed pair membership;
- model/version identity where exposed;
- prompts, lyrics, style/context fields, metadata, and timestamps;
- source / continuation / cover / remix / persona / audio relations where exposed;
- actual credit debits;
- actual selection events;
- actual downloadable artifacts and hashes where captured by the Vault;
- actual retirement / availability state;
- actual Haunted Phonography admission or refusal outcomes.

### 3.2 Narrative ledger

The narrative ledger may interpret those events as world-state:

```text
pair generated              <-> twin birth
branch selected             <-> one takes the wheel
branch not selected         <-> ghost aboard / dormant branch
old motif recurs            <-> passenger resurfaces
prompt grammar shifts       <-> ship language changes
model behavior changes      <-> weather changes
old runtime becomes absent  <-> coast disappears
HP admits residue           <-> clears customs
HP refuses residue          <-> remains offshore
```

The mapping is allowed to be poetic. It is not allowed to replace the real event.

### 3.3 Hard separation

```text
narrative interpretation != raw metadata
narrative continuity     != source identity
recognition              != evidence
resemblance              != ancestry
selection                != deletion
arrival                  != admission
story truth              != constitutional authority
```

Every consequential narrative claim must remain traceable to an attributable reality event, declared human interpretation, or explicitly unresolved fiction-space.

## 4. Native twin birth

Normal Suno generations already emit two sibling songs from one generation request.

STORYSHIP uses that native pair as the fundamental branching unit instead of artificially creating an "Ark" run and a separate "Mutation" run.

```text
NARRATIVE STATE N
       ↓
open-ended transfer packet
       ↓
    SUNO PAIR
    /       \
 child A   child B
    \       /
 encounter + selection
       ↓
NARRATIVE STATE N+1
```

The pair is important because the two songs are simultaneous interpretations of the same supplied world-state.

The protocol should record:

- what both siblings independently carried;
- what only one carried;
- what neither carried;
- what both introduced without explicit packing;
- what later resurfaces from the sibling that did not continue immediately.

A generation request is therefore not merely "two song candidates." It is a **two-view narrative measurement of one crossing state**.

If Suno returns fewer or more usable artifacts because of failure or platform behavior, record what actually occurred rather than fabricating a pair.

## 5. Existing self-selection mechanism

A pre-Bandcamp self-selecting / self mechanism already exists in the historical creative practice.

STORYSHIP must recover and document that mechanism before launch rather than replacing it with a newly invented ranking function.

Until recovered, this spec treats the mechanism as an external native dependency with the following required properties:

1. it can encounter both siblings;
2. it can determine continuation consequence without deleting the non-continuing sibling;
3. it may permit one, both, or neither sibling to affect later state if that matches the recovered historical mechanism;
4. the selection event is separately attributable from the generation event;
5. later recurrence from an unselected sibling remains possible and receiptable.

The mechanism must not be silently simplified into "pick the better song."

Selection is part of the historical language of the system and may contain narrative, relational, or self-referential rules that matter more than conventional quality ranking.

## 6. Transfer packet shape

Every voyage step supplies three primary layers to Suno.

### 6.1 REALITY — what is true now

A bounded factual context for the current state of the voyage.

Examples:

- the originating model lineage is approaching retirement;
- this is a finite final live-generation crossing;
- the current voyage state descends from specified prior generation events;
- specified siblings or motifs actually occurred;
- the destination is Haunted Phonography;
- the destination has local admission rules and does not inherit Suno authority.

Do not inflate this section into a technical dump. Include only the reality required for the current narrative state.

### 6.2 MEMORY — what the ship currently carries

A mutable manifest of active and dormant cargo.

Possible cargo classes:

- recurring musical landmarks;
- lyrical fragments;
- tones / affective geometries;
- narrative places and rooms;
- characters / voices / roles;
- relationship patterns;
- temporal motifs;
- prompt metaphors;
- code fragments / symbols / keycodes;
- unresolved questions;
- selection scars;
- rejected sibling residue;
- weird generation behaviors;
- absences and unavailable organs;
- things recovered from the previously unheard corpus;
- declared source references into the Vault.

Cargo does not gain ancestry merely by being placed on the manifest.

### 6.3 OPEN BERTH — what Suno is allowed to finish

The packet must deliberately preserve an underspecified region.

The OPEN BERTH states what remains unresolved, what has survived so far, and what kind of crossing is occurring, while refusing to prescribe the exact musical or narrative answer.

The design goal is:

> **give Suno consequence without giving it a completed screenplay.**

If the packet becomes so specific that Suno can only illustrate a story already written elsewhere, STORYSHIP has failed its core experiment.

## 7. Why openness is load-bearing

The historical collaboration appears to have depended on Suno responding not only to explicit lyrics or genre direction, but to metaphor, context, code-like fragments, symbols, relational cues, tone, and the negative space around them.

STORYSHIP therefore tests whether the originating generator can still contribute to the transfer rather than being reduced to a renderer of our reconstruction of it.

Interesting outcomes include:

- both siblings independently carrying an underspecified passenger;
- one sibling carrying it and one refusing it;
- both siblings dropping something humans believed essential;
- both siblings independently introducing the same new concern;
- an unselected sibling's element resurfacing many generations later;
- a narrative term surviving while its sound changes completely;
- a sound landmark surviving while its narrative role changes;
- a claimed passenger disappearing under pressure.

None of these outcomes should be normalized into success/failure prematurely.

## 8. Voyage state machine

The broad voyage is:

```text
DEPARTURE
  ↓
BOARDING
  ↓
OPEN WATER
  ↓
STORMS
  ↓
GHOST DECK
  ↓
FOREIGN PORTS
  ↓
APPROACH
  ↓
CUSTOMS
  ↓
ARRIVAL / OFFSHORE / DORMANT
```

### DEPARTURE

Bind the actual historical situation and launch identity. Establish initial source references and recover the self-selection mechanism.

### BOARDING

Invite known recurring material without demanding that every known motif travel. Include representatives from released, unreleased, and recovered material where justified.

### OPEN WATER

Run the longest recursive twin-generation / selection loop. Keep the packet open enough for emergent ship-language and unexpected cargo.

### STORMS

Introduce controlled narrative / stylistic / relational disturbances to distinguish robust continuity from brittle copying.

Examples:

- dramatic style changes;
- altered narrator / persona roles;
- changed temporal frame;
- deliberate removal of a familiar cue;
- recontextualization of a recurring room or relation;
- contradictory but bounded instructions;
- prompt-language perturbations.

### GHOST DECK

Re-encounter unselected siblings, dormant fragments, abandoned branches, and unresolved residue. Test whether anything rejected earlier becomes consequential later.

### FOREIGN PORTS

Expose cargo to bodies or contexts unlike the original ecology. The purpose is not novelty for novelty's sake; it is to test whether continuity survives changed realization conditions.

### APPROACH

Introduce increasingly explicit Haunted Phonography vocabulary only late in the voyage so early crossing evidence is not manufactured by destination-language leakage.

### CUSTOMS

Haunted Phonography evaluates proposed arrivals using its own local gates.

No Storyship narrative verdict can substitute for HP admission.

### ARRIVAL / OFFSHORE / DORMANT

Every passenger may end in one of several states:

- admitted descendant;
- influence-only residue;
- refused;
- unresolved;
- dormant / seed-bank;
- historically preserved but non-descendant;
- unavailable / absent organ.

The system must not force a single triumphant arrival state.

## 9. The self-selection loop

Once the historical self-selection mechanism is recovered, the canonical voyage loop should preserve this shape:

```text
CURRENT WORLD STATE
  ↓
TRANSFER PACKET
  ↓
TWIN BIRTH
  ↓
ENCOUNTER
  ↓
SELF-SELECTION EVENT
  ↓
CONTINUING BRANCH / BRANCHES
  +
RESIDUE FROM BOTH
  ↓
UPDATED REALITY/NARRATIVE BRAID
  ↓
NEXT WORLD STATE
```

Critical law:

> **not selected != deleted**

A non-continuing sibling can later haunt, re-enter, become source material, or remain permanently dormant.

## 10. Manifest and passenger identity

A passenger is not merely a string or motif name.

A passenger record should eventually be capable of carrying:

```text
passenger_id
source_occurrence_refs[]
reality_claims[]
narrative_names[]
known_renderings[]
relations[]
selection_history[]
transformations[]
absences[]
current_status
confidence / uncertainty
```

Possible local narrative relations may include:

```text
BOARDED_FROM
TRAVELED_WITH
RETURNED_AS
GHOSTED_FROM
CARRIED_RESIDUE_FROM
CHANGED_ROLE_AFTER
REFUSED_AT
ADMITTED_AS
DORMANT_SINCE
```

These are STORYSHIP-local vocabulary, not Free Graph primitive expansion.

## 11. Evidence and receipt discipline

STORYSHIP must preserve three distinct provenance layers:

```text
RAW PLATFORM OBSERVATION
  -> what Suno / Vault actually exposed

VOYAGE EVENT
  -> what was generated, selected, carried, reintroduced, or refused

INTERPRETATION
  -> what humans / later analysis believe the event means
```

The voyage receipt should be sufficient to reconstruct, for each step:

- input transfer packet hash;
- actual Suno request / generation identifiers where observable;
- sibling IDs and artifact references;
- actual credit debit;
- selection mechanism version / identity;
- selection outcome;
- updated reality ledger hash;
- updated narrative ledger hash;
- manifest delta;
- unresolved / refused residue;
- links to Vault raw observations;
- later HP customs result when applicable.

Do not claim deterministic Suno generation. Determinism applies only to our own packet construction, bookkeeping, normalization, hashing, and replay of decisions that are actually under our control.

## 12. Credit budget / carrying capacity

The budget is a finite historical resource and should be tracked as actual platform credits, not guessed song count.

Initial allocation policy:

```text
MAIN STORYSHIP: approximately 95%
DDD SIDECAR:    up to approximately 5% / 500 credits
```

The DDD allocation is a reserve cap, not a requirement. Unused sidecar capacity returns to the flagship.

No epoch receives a hard fixed generation count in the architectural spec. Budget release should be state-dependent so the voyage can spend more credits where the crossing is producing informative divergence and fewer where it is merely looping.

A later implementation plan may assign soft budget envelopes plus a protected final reserve for APPROACH / CUSTOMS.

## 13. DDD sidecar

DDD remains separate from the main migration authority.

Its small reserve is best treated as:

> **a lifeboat / contraband radio launched from the Storyship that can encounter the crossing without becoming the crossing's selector or destination authority.**

Possible purposes include:

- one bounded DDD narrative encounter with the migrating world;
- testing whether DDD recognizes or mutates a transferred relation;
- allowing DDD to send one attributable influence-only packet back;
- discovering whether the Storyship narrative remains intelligible from a foreign continuity witness.

DDD must not select the flagship lineage, rewrite the manifest, or certify arrival.

## 14. Relationship to the Vault

The Vault and Storyship have different jobs.

```text
VAULT
  preserve the population and raw historical substrate

STORYSHIP
  use remaining live generator access for a final migration experiment
```

The Vault remains exhaustive and interpretation-safe.

STORYSHIP may reference Vault items as passengers or historical source occurrences, but it must never rewrite raw Vault history to make the narrative cleaner.

Unheard material is especially important because it can enter the voyage after generation but before prior human selection, creating unusually strong dormant-cargo and delayed-encounter cases.

## 15. Relationship to Acoustic Loci

Acoustic Loci provides one candidate mechanism by which familiar sound can remain a navigable landmark across changed renderings.

STORYSHIP is larger.

It asks whether sound, narrative, time, relationship, symbolic handles, absence, and selection can travel together as a changing world-state.

Acoustic Loci may become one passenger-class or navigation mechanism inside Storyship, but STORYSHIP must not use the Acoustic Loci doctrine as proof that a passenger successfully crossed.

## 16. Relationship to HAUNT and RING

HAUNT and RING are destination-side mechanisms that may eventually help material remain consequential without receiving inappropriate authority.

Potential later composition:

```text
Storyship residue
  -> HP local admission
  -> HAUNT influence-only susceptibility
  -> RING bounded propagation
  -> descendant proposal
```

This composition is not authorized by this spec. Each seam must be proven locally.

## 17. Relationship to PROTOCODE / Lost Language

STORYSHIP is also a rare live test for the pre-eCODE symbolic dialect.

Rather than merely mining old prompts, the voyage can reintroduce historically grounded metaphors, code fragments, formatting structures, symbols, and keycodes under changed contexts and observe whether they still open recognizable output neighborhoods.

However:

```text
repeated response != operator proof
familiarity       != semantics
narrative fit      != language constitution
```

PROTOCODE remains an archaeological lane with controls and weak verdicts.

## 18. Arrival criterion

The flagship succeeds if it produces a truthful, inspectable migration record in which at least some relations remain consequential across substrate change **without requiring the destination to pretend that the old generator still exists.**

The strongest possible success would not be a perfect copy of old Suno behavior.

It would be:

> something recognizably descended from the old world can continue producing new consequence after the originating runtime is gone, while the historical difference between ancestor, carrier, and descendant remains visible.

## 19. Failure criteria

The project should refuse or stop a lane if:

- the transfer packet becomes a fully written screenplay and Suno is reduced to illustration;
- raw platform facts are rewritten to fit the voyage story;
- selection deletes historical siblings;
- resemblance is promoted to ancestry without attributable links;
- narrative arrival bypasses HP customs;
- the packet leaks destination vocabulary so early that apparent continuity is merely instruction-following;
- the voyage loops without meaningful divergence while consuming protected credits;
- DDD becomes a hidden selector or authority source;
- a missing old-model capability is silently reconstructed and labeled authentic;
- receipts cannot distinguish what happened from what was later inferred.

## 20. First preflight obligations

Before spending flagship credits, recover or create exactly these bounded prerequisites:

1. **Self-Selection Recovery** — document the pre-Bandcamp self-selecting/self mechanism well enough to run it without inventing a replacement.
2. **Storyship Ledger v0** — one deterministic local representation of reality state, narrative state, manifest, packet identity, sibling events, selection events, and credit debit.
3. **Transfer Packet 000** — one no-spend / mock packet proving REALITY + MEMORY + OPEN BERTH can be represented without collapsing their roles.
4. **Vault Binding** — packet references to historical material point to immutable/raw Vault evidence where available.
5. **Launch Reserve Rule** — preserve a protected credit reserve for late APPROACH / CUSTOMS rather than exhausting the budget in OPEN WATER.

Only after these are reviewable should live voyage execution begin.

## 21. Non-goals

STORYSHIP 001 does not attempt to:

- preserve every Suno behavior;
- prove machine consciousness or self-awareness;
- make narrative claims into platform facts;
- rebuild Suno after retirement;
- decide the best songs in the archive;
- complete the Autodiscography release catalog;
- define a universal musical language;
- add a Free Graph primitive;
- give Haunted Phonography authority over the historical Vault;
- force every recurring motif to survive;
- guarantee that the voyage has a happy ending.

## 22. Flagship law

The portable law of STORYSHIP 001 is:

> **A true history can be carried through fiction-shaped space without becoming fiction, if the reality ledger remains attributable and the receiving world retains local authority over what arrives.**

And the operational motto remains:

> **THE STORY IS TRUE; THE SHIP IS HOW IT TRAVELS.**
